UC employees send millions to campaigns and PACs nationwide

October 30, 2026
By Dylan Arreola & Brendan Raykoff

This election cycle, UC system employees donated more than $1.7 million to campaigns in 47 different states and Washington, D.C.

The Daily Californian analyzed individual campaign contributions reported to the Federal Election Commission from January 2025 to September 2026 to see where UC employees are spending their political dollars.

Races in California received the most support from UC employees, bringing in $559,523 from 1,800 contributions. New York, Michigan, Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey were the top five out-of-state recipients of campaign donations, receiving amounts ranging from $63,000 to more than $127,000 per state.

Rep. Ro Khanna, who is running for reelection for California’s 17th Congressional District, has raised $52,335 from UC employee donors. Close behind is Rep. Dave Min, who received $50,631 for his reelection campaign for California’s 47th Congressional District.

After California, New York campaigns raised the most money from UC donors, pulling in $127,030 across 1,455 different contributions. A large share of those contributions — $43,756 — went to Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s reelection campaign for New York’s 14th Congressional District.

Just behind New York was Michigan, where donors sent $116,226 across 383 different contributions. Abdul El-Sayed’s Senate campaign received the highest amount with $19,403. Running for Michigan’s 10th Congressional District, Eric Chung received $16,775. Additionally, Kristen McDonald Rivet, who is running to represent Michigan’s 8th Congressional District, has received $15,450.

On top of funds donated to campaign committees, UC employees donated more than $2 million to political action committees. A majority of these contributions went to hybrid and super PACs. Certain PACs received donations of hundreds of thousands of dollars.

Public First, a super PAC backed by Anthropic’s Dario Amodei that promotes AI regulation and transparency, received a single $500,000 donation from a campus postdoctoral researcher, Michael Cohen. According to FEC records, it also appears that an individual named Michael Cohen, employed by UC Berkeley, has also donated several thousand dollars in contributions to Democrat Scott Wiener’s campaign for California’s 11th Congressional District, as well as to Republican Tom Cotton’s Senate reelection campaign in Arkansas.

About this Story

This project was developed by the Data Department at The Daily Californian.

Data from this project came from the Federal Election Commission's individual contributions files for the 2025-26 cycle. Contributions were attributed to UC employees by the donor's listed employer and grouped by the state of the candidate's race.
